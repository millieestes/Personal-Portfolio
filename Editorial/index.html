Collaborate Book Design

A collaborative book design project that explores the intersection of Aesop’s fables and visual storytelling. Our group reimagined the classic fables through illustration, typography, and layout, creating a cohesive book that brings each story to life through a modern visual approach.

This is Water

Book Design based on David Foster Wallace's speech This is Water

A book design based on David Foster Wallace's speech This Is Water, using a single typeface to explore how typography, layout, and pacing can communicate the ideas and emotions of the speech.

Icons for Awareness

Visual communication & awareness

A visual communication project exploring the effects of industrialization through a series of icons. Each icon uses simplified imagery to represent different aspects of industrialization and communicate the topic in a clear, accessible way.

Ode to the Plate

Book Design based on Pablo Neruda's Odes to Common Things

A book design based on Pablo Neruda's Odes to Common Things, inspired by a plate passed down from my great grandmother. The design explores the memories and meaning held in an everyday object, using typography, imagery, and layout to turn something familiar into a personal visual story.

Typographic Grid

Book Design

A book design project exploring the use of typographic grids to create structure, hierarchy, and consistency. The design uses typography, spacing, and layout to create a cohesive visual system throughout the book.
